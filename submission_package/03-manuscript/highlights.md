# Highlights

- Systematic PRISMA review of 39 studies (88% from the last three years) on AI tools and founder decision-making in early-stage startups.
- The evidence base is contested, not settled: 15 studies support positive effects on decision speed/quality, 7 oppose or report null results, 10 are mixed.
- Convergent evidence shows AI compresses founders' information-gathering and evaluation cycles; opposing evidence concentrates where decisions demand ambiguous human judgment.
- A contingency perspective derives testable boundary conditions (task structure, founder digital capability, contextual support) that reconcile the divergent findings.
- Full transparency package: review matrix, stance data, claim-verification reports, and provenance records accompany the manuscript.
